How Many Classes Can I Skip?

Use this to plan leave without dropping below your required attendance.

Step by step

  • Write down classes attended (A) and total classes held (T).
  • Divide A by 0.75 (use 0.80 for an 80% rule).
  • Subtract T.
  • Round down. That is how many more classes you can miss.

Example

A = 45, T = 60. 45 ÷ 0.75 = 60. 60 − 60 = 0. You are right at the limit and cannot skip the next class.

Multi-hour classes

A lab or a 3-hour class counts as 3 classes in AttendTrack, because each hour is held and counted. Check how your college counts them.
