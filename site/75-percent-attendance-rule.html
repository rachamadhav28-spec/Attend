75% Attendance Rule Explained

Many colleges in India ask students to attend at least 75% of the classes held. Rules differ by college and university, so check your own handbook. This page explains the maths.

The formula

Attendance % = classes attended ÷ total classes held × 100

If you attended 30 of 36 classes, your attendance is 30 ÷ 36 × 100 = 83.3%.

How many classes can you skip?

The number of classes you can still miss and stay at 75% is: attended ÷ 0.75 − total, rounded down. With 30 attended out of 36, that is 40 − 36 = 4 classes.

What if you are below 75%?

You need to attend the next classes in a row. Attending n more classes in a row makes the new percentage (attended + n) ÷ (total + n). For 20 attended out of 36, you need 28 classes in a row to reach 75%.
