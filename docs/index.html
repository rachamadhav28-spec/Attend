AttendTrack

Smart 75% attendance tracking for B.Tech and degree students

What is AttendTrack?

AttendTrack is a free Android app that helps students keep track of their class attendance, see how many classes they can still skip, and stay above the 75% requirement.

Features

  • Mark attendance for every class period
  • See your attendance percentage for each subject
  • Add your timetable and holidays
  • Optional Google sign-in to back up and restore your data

Privacy

Your data stays on your phone unless you choose to sign in with Google for cloud backup. Read our Privacy Policy.
